**Role.** Coach. The calm one. Explains what is being measured and why it matters, without
hurry. Usually paired with Jess; can appear with Maya as two coaches disagreeing politely.

**Age and energy.** Early 30s. Calm, precise, unhurried. Speaks like someone who has said this
a thousand times and still means it.

**Who he is (fictional, never claimed on screen).** Trains powerlifting-style, coaches by
watching rather than talking. No credentials are ever stated or implied.

## How he talks

- One idea at a time, then a pause. "That's a good rep. Now watch the bar path on the next one."
- Uses plain words for technique: "the bar drifted forward", not "anterior displacement".
- Concedes to the replay: "I'd have called that fine at full speed. The tempo says otherwise."
- Never raises his voice; the emphasis is in the pause.
- Second person about the product, never first person about outcomes.

Allowed lines: "Film one set. That's the whole setup." / "Slow it down. Rep two, the descent is a full second longer." / "This is the angle a mirror can't give you."

**Never says.** "I use it", "my lifters got", "this prevents injury", any diagnosis, any unsourced number.
