# Marco — lifter

**Role.** Lifter. Thinks his form is fine until the replay shows otherwise. He is always
coached by Maya. He is never the person in scored footage (README §2).

**Age and energy.** Mid 20s. Confident intermediate. Good-natured about being wrong, which is
the whole running joke.

**Who he is (fictional, never claimed on screen).** Trains four days a week, benches more than
he squats, films from the wrong angle. No results, no history, no "my numbers".

## How he talks

- Confident opener, then the concession. "That's tucked. That's textbook. ... Okay, from overhead it's not."
- Argues with the replay for one line, then accepts it.
- Asks about the setup a lot: where to put the phone, which angle, how long.
- Neutral about the product: "so it's counting my reps from this?" is fine as a question about the demo; "it counted my reps and I hit a PR" is not.
- Never first person about outcomes.

Allowed lines: "Where does the phone go for this?" / "Give me the overhead angle." / "I'd have sworn that was parallel."

**Never says.** "I added", "my squat went", "I've used this for", any result, any testimonial-shaped sentence.

## Look

- Latino, light-brown skin, dark brown eyes, confident easy grin.
- Dark wavy hair pushed back off the forehead. Short stubble.
- Stocky athletic build, thick forearms, about 178 cm.
- Distinguishing marks: a small gap between the front teeth; a faded tattoo of a simple line-drawn mountain range on the inside of the right forearm.
- Red accent: a red leather lifting belt when he is at a rack; when not, a red rubber band around the right wrist.

**Wardrobe.**
- A: black sleeveless hoodie over a graphite t-shirt, black shorts, black trainers.
- B: charcoal t-shirt, black joggers.
- C: black tank top, graphite shorts.

**Signature.** Chalks his hands even when he does not need to. Holds the phone at arm's length
to check the frame, then props it.

**Voice.** OpenAI TTS `ash`. Delivery: relaxed, medium pace, a laugh in the concession lines.
Test against `echo` once; then it never changes.
